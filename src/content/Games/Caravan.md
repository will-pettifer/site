### Rules
Players take it in turns to place or move cards into one of three zones that face off. The game ends when each face off has a valid hand. The player with the most valid hands wins. A valid hand is either:

- Between 25 and 30, inclusive, and higher than the opposing hand, or,
- Exactly 5 below the opposing hand.

If the game ends, it will reset after 10 seconds.
